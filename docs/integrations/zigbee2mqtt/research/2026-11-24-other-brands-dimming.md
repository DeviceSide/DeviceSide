# Other brands: dimming (raise, lower, stop) on Zigbee lights

2026-11-24. Research for whether `other`'s Labs level on Zigbee2MQTT's `raise_brightness`, `lower_brightness` and `stop` holds. Today it rests on one sentence: a Zigbee 3.0 certified dimmable light must take Level Control's Move, Move to Level and Stop ([docs/internals.md](../../../internals.md#shared)). This note tests it against what DeviceSide publishes, what zigbee-herdsman-converters (ZHC) turns that into, and what is reported on the vendors `other` holds. It follows [the companion on set and get](2026-11-24-other-brands-execute-if-off-and-reads.md); anything not confirmed is marked unverified.

Sources pinned: ZHC `03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26` (same as the companion); zigbee2mqtt (Z2M) `3c841fdfd66c92239921ef84fde8f039c64e1662`; ZCL Revision 8 text from [the Athom mirror](https://etc.athom.com/zigbee_cluster_specification.pdf), read with `pdftotext -layout` (the CSA copy blocks scripts; [the earlier ZCL note](../../../research/2026-11-17-zcl-rev8-level-and-onoff.md) read the CSA PDF by hand). Issue searches by `gh` on 2026-11-24 over Z2M, ZHC, zigpy/zha, zigpy/zha-device-handlers and home-assistant/core; GitHub's search is weak and rate-limited, so a missing report is "not found", not "does not exist". No hardware was touched: **no rig record exists for any non-Hue light on these actions** (`docs/integrations/zigbee2mqtt/test-records/raise_brightness.json` holds Hue bulbs only).

## Synthesis

- **Verdict: safe in Labs as-is; no guard, nothing to pull.** Nothing found makes a dimming call leave a light in a state the user didn't ask for and can't see: every departure found shows at once and one tap undoes it ([docs/evidence.md](../../../evidence.md), "When today's call harms a class": dimming and `stop` don't harm). The call is also not a new one for `other`: for almost every vendor in it ZHC builds the same `moveToLevelWithOnOff` and `move`/`stop` it builds for Hue (Part 1).
- **The stated reason is thinner than the verdict.** The ZCL part holds (Part 2): Move to Level, Move and Stop are all mandatory in Level Control, and the spec lets a device that can't fade at a variable rate "disregard" the transition, so a light that jumps still conforms. But nothing shows these vendors' lights are certified, and nothing positive was found either: no rig record, and no report of a long fade working on Innr, Ledvance/Osram, Paulmann, Nanoleaf, EcoDim or Hornbach/flair. The evidence for Labs is "same standard call as Hue, a few model-level departures, none harmful", not certification. Suggest the internals entry say so.
- **No departure reported for Aqara, Innr, Paulmann, Nanoleaf, EcoDim, Hornbach/flair on the dimming call.** An Aqara T1 strip's brightness transitions work (the reporter's test, [Z2M #31128](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/31128)); its color transitions don't, which DeviceSide's brightness fades don't use. Innr turns off at level 1, which ZHC already compensates on 92 definitions (Part 3).
- **Departures found, all model- or firmware-scoped and visible at once:**
  - **Müller Licht tint, older firmware** (45327 `MGU10501`, 45328 `MC37501`, 404065 `MDFIL201`): a turn-on transition to a level below 103 flashes and turns off again; closed stale, never fixed ([Z2M #15832](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/15832)). This is the strongest evidence against raise-from-off on `other`. Untested with DeviceSide's transition of 0 at level 1 (Part 4a).
  - **AwoX/Eglo**: a soft off (`state OFF` with a transition) ends at level 1 and the light stays on; ZHC fixes it for one model (EBF_RGB_Zm) with a local converter, and `lower_brightness` to 0 on the other AwoX lights is exposed ([Z2M #19007](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/19007), [Z2M #31956](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/31956)).
  - **Ledvance strip 4058075729384/4058075729148**: the transition is ignored from off to on and honored otherwise ([Z2M #20043](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/20043)). DeviceSide fades on to on, so it doesn't meet this (Part 4a).
  - **Lidl/Livarno and other Tuya-inside bulbs**: levels 1 and 2 leave the bulb on while it reports off; some ignore `moveToLevelWithOnOff`. These are not in `other` once placed (they match `tuya_zcl`, not built for dimming), but are during the window before Z2M's bridge/devices names them (Part 6).
- **Plugs and relays can't reach it.** The three adapters serve the `light` domain only; core drops `brightness` for a light whose state lists no brightness mode, and a `switch_as_x` light is served as the switch it wraps (Part 5). A relay that Z2M shows as an on/off light gets `unchanged` with `fields_dropped` on a raise or lower, and a harmless unanswered `brightness_move` on a stop.
- **Worth knowing, not worth a guard:** ZHC's stop reads the light back after 500 ms and throws if the read fails, so a delivered stop can be reported as failed; Z2M reports a stop's state unreliably on a LEDVANCE panel ([Z2M #33321](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/33321)); an Innr member in a mixed group isn't given the level-1-to-2 fix (ZHC `allEqual`, inferred, unverified); a Z2M maintainer calls Z2M's simulated off transition "not guaranteed to work" ([Z2M #31956](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/31956)).
- **Could not verify:** every vendor's behavior on the DeviceSide call on hardware; certification of any listed product; Müller's behavior at transition 0; Stop failing on any non-Hue light (no report either way). Details at the end.

## Part 1: What DeviceSide publishes and what ZHC sends

### 1a. DeviceSide's payloads

From `custom_components/deviceside/integrations/zigbee2mqtt/fade.py` (`AT_THE_BOTTOM`, `brightness_fade`), `stop.py` and [internals.md](../internals.md) (`fade`, `stop`):

| Call | Z2M `/set` payload |
|---|---|
| raise, light on | `{"state": "ON", "brightness": N, "transition": T}`, N = `max(1, round(limit/100*254))`, T rounded to 0.1 s |
| raise, light off | first `{"state": "ON", "brightness": 1, "transition": 0}` (one publish to an all-off group, else per off member), then the line above |
| lower to a limit above 0 | `{"brightness": N, "transition": T}` (no `state`) |
| lower to 0 | `{"state": "OFF", "transition": T}` (Z2M's soft off) |
| stop | `{"brightness_move": "stop"}`, first `hue_move` / a raw `stopMoveStep` where a color move of ours runs |

Reads at the end of a fade (`{"state": "", "brightness": ""}` to `/get`) and after a stop are Z2M's `convertGet`.

### 1b. Conversion in ZHC

- **Every light definition of `other` shares one converter set.** `modernExtend.light` puts `tz.light_onoff_brightness`, `tz.ignore_transition`, `tz.light_brightness_move` and `tz.light_brightness_step` on any light with brightness ([modernExtend.ts L1341-L1347](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/lib/modernExtend.ts#L1341)). Aqara's `lumiLight` wraps it unchanged and adds only vendor attributes ([lib/lumi.ts L2846-L2880](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/lib/lumi.ts#L2846)); Tuya's `tuyaLight` wraps it too ([lib/tuya.ts L5034-L5100](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/lib/tuya.ts#L5034)). Ledvance, Osram, Innr, Müller, Paulmann, Nanoleaf, Hornbach and EcoDim definitions are `m.light(...)` or small wrappers over it ([innr.ts](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/devices/innr.ts), [hornbach.ts](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/devices/hornbach.ts), [nanoleaf.ts](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/devices/nanoleaf.ts), [ecodim.ts](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/devices/ecodim.ts), read, 2026-11-24). The definitions in `src/devices` and `src/lib` that wrap or re-list it (rg `light_onoff_brightness`): AwoX EBF_RGB_Zm, Tuya TS110E, Namron, Legrand, Centralite, Siglis, Sunricher, Inovelli, Busch-Jaeger, dresden elektronik; of the vendors named in the question, only AwoX changes what it does for these payloads (Part 3).
- **`{"state":"ON","brightness":N,"transition":T}`** goes to `light_onoff_brightness`: with a brightness given it ends in `genLevelCtrl.moveToLevelWithOnOff` with `level` N, `transtime` = T x 10 (tenths of a second) and `optionsMask: 0, optionsOverride: 0` ([toZigbee.ts L1252-L1424](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/converters/toZigbee.ts#L1252), the command at [L1402-L1410](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/converters/toZigbee.ts#L1402); transition parsing in [utils.ts getTransition L497-L528](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/lib/utils.ts#L497)). Level 1 is raised to 2 when the definition sets `turnsOffAtBrightness1` ([L1378](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/converters/toZigbee.ts#L1378)). Without `state` (lower to a limit) ZHC infers `state` from the level, so it is the same `moveToLevelWithOnOff` ([L1291-L1300](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/converters/toZigbee.ts#L1291)). Z2M calls the converter once for `state`, `brightness` and `transition` together ([publish.ts L202](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/blob/3c841fdfd66c92239921ef84fde8f039c64e1662/lib/extension/publish.ts#L202)). Read from the code; no wire capture here.
- **`{"state":"OFF","transition":T}`** is ZHC's "simulate Off with a transition": `brightness = 0`, then `moveToLevelWithOnOff` level 0 over T ([L1305-L1311](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/converters/toZigbee.ts#L1305)); with no transition it is a plain `genOnOff.off`. The `transition` key is swallowed by `tz.ignore_transition`.
- **`{"brightness_move":"stop"}`** is `genLevelCtrl.stop` with the zero option fields, then a 500 ms sleep and two reads (`genOnOff.onOff`, `genLevelCtrl.currentLevel`) whose result becomes the published state ([toZigbee.ts L986-L1012](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/converters/toZigbee.ts#L986)). The reads follow the command: a light that doesn't answer them makes the converter throw after the stop went out (code reading; unverified on hardware). For a group, the reads go to the first member (`getEntityOrFirstGroupMember`), as [internals.md](../internals.md) already notes.
- **Order.** Z2M sorts a message so state and brightness go last for a light that is on and first for one that is off ([publish.ts L145-L156](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/blob/3c841fdfd66c92239921ef84fde8f039c64e1662/lib/extension/publish.ts#L145)); DeviceSide's messages hold only those keys plus `transition`, so the sort changes nothing.
- **Per-model overrides of this path in ZHC:** `turnsOffAtBrightness1` (Part 3), `moveToLevelWithOnOffDisable` (5 Tuya definitions plus `modernExtend.light`'s argument; `light_onoff_brightness` then sends plain `moveToLevel` and `on`/`off` separately, [L1387-L1403](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/converters/toZigbee.ts#L1387)), `noOffTransitionWhenOff` (2 Tuya definitions and IKEA firmware above 1.0.012, [lib/ikea.ts L129](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/lib/ikea.ts#L129)) and `omitOptionalLevelAndColorParams` (Paul Neuhaus x10, Schneider, Ribag; the companion's Part 1). None of the first three is set on a vendor in the question except Innr's (and Lumi's three Opple lights, Gledopto's and Hue's, which are other families or Hue).

## Part 2: What the spec requires (ZCL Revision 8)

Text extract of the mirror, sections as numbered there:

- **The commands are mandatory.** Move to Level, Move, Step, Stop and their With On/Off forms are all M in Level Control (3.10.2.3, Table 3-59) and in Level Control for Lighting (Table 3-145), as [the earlier note](../../../research/2026-11-17-zcl-rev8-level-and-onoff.md) records from the CSA PDF.
- **A transition can be disregarded.** Move to Level (3.10.2.3.1.2): the time taken "SHALL be equal to the value of the Transition time field, in tenths of a second, or as close to this as the device is able"; "If the device is not able to move at a variable rate, the Transition time field MAY be disregarded." So a light that jumps (the Ledvance strip off to on, the Lidl presets in Part 4a) is conformant, and DeviceSide's "the bulb runs the fade" assumption can fail without a defect. Transition 0xFFFF means the OnOffTransitionTime attribute; DeviceSide never sends it (T is clamped to 6553.4 s, [docs/internals.md](../../../internals.md#shared)).
- **Stop** (3.10.2.3.4.2): any Move to Level, Move or Step "(and their 'with On/Off' variants) currently in process SHALL be terminated. The value of CurrentLevel SHALL be left at its value upon receipt of the Stop command". The Stop is identical for both sets "because the dependency on On/Off is determined by the original command that is being stopped." So a conforming light halts a fade where it is, which is what DeviceSide's Hue lab probe shows ([internals.md](../internals.md), `stop`).
- **Level 0 and the minimum.** For a lighting device a CurrentLevel of 0 "SHALL not be used" and 1 is the minimum attainable (3.19.2.2.1). A With On/Off command that reaches the minimum turns the light off (3.10.2.1.2; Table 3-146, "Move with On/Off ... MIN ... Off"). So the soft off, a `moveToLevelWithOnOff` to 0 over T, is spec-conformant only in that a light clamps it to the minimum and turns off on arrival; ZHC's own comment calls it a simulation, and Part 4c shows lights that stay on at level 1.
- **Without On/Off while off.** A plain `moveToLevel` to an off light is ignored unless ExecuteIfOff is set (Table 3-146; the companion's Part 1). DeviceSide's lower to a limit sends a level with no `state` to a light that is on, so this doesn't arise; a raise from off uses the With On/Off form (`state: ON`), which the spec says turns the light on.
- **Certification.** That a Zigbee 3.0 certified dimmable light takes these is as in [docs/internals.md](../../../internals.md#shared) (NXP's device-type table, Silicon Labs' certification page). Whether any given product here is certified: **unverified** for every vendor in Part 3. ZLO/ZLL-only older bulbs predate ZCL 7, but the commands used here predate ZCL 7 as far as the companion's revision-history reading goes (Options is the only new part, and DeviceSide's dimming sends it as zeros); not checked against ZCL 6 text: unverified.

## Part 4: Reported departures, by segment of the call

### 4a. Transition ignored or from off

- **Jumps instead of fading:** Ledvance strip, off to on only ([Z2M #20043](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/20043)); Lidl bulbs with a fixed short fade ([Z2M #5175](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/5175)); Aqara T1 strip, color only ([Z2M #31128](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/31128)). The spec allows it (Part 2). Cost to DeviceSide: a long fade becomes a jump, and its predicted brightness (a line from start to end over T) is wrong until the end read-back. Visible; no harm. How far DeviceSide's fade history of Hue bulbs applies to a jumping bulb: unverified.
- **Raise from off is built to avoid a transition from off.** DeviceSide first turns the light on at level 1 with transition 0, then fades on to on, so the Ledvance case doesn't arise (design, [internals.md](../internals.md) `fade`). The first step has two exposures. (1) Level 1 must not read as off: Innr is covered by `turnsOffAtBrightness1`; a Lidl/Tuya TS0505B holds 1 and 2 as "off" (Part 6). (2) The `moveToLevelWithOnOff` to a low level from off must not flash and turn off. **Müller tint reports exactly that** at a 4 s transition below 103 ([Z2M #15832](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/15832)); the reporter says it works "without transition". ZHC's no-transition path for an ON with a brightness is the same `moveToLevelWithOnOff` with `transtime` 0 (L1402-L1410), so DeviceSide's step 1 is closer to the working case, but nobody tested that: **unverified**. If step 1 flashes off, step 2 is a fade from off, which the report says also flashes below 103.
- **Aqara `transition` setting** ([Z2M #29987](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/29987)) and Innr/Aqara CL-L02D in [Z2M #29012](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/29012) are about `{"state":"ON"}` with a configured transition and no brightness, where ZHC reads `onLevel` and resends "previous" ([L1331-L1363](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/converters/toZigbee.ts#L1331)). DeviceSide always sends a brightness, so the path isn't taken. It would be if a user set a per-device `transition`, which affects DeviceSide's own sends only through the explicit `transition` DeviceSide sends.

### 4b. Turns off or sticks at low levels

- Innr: level 1 is off; fixed in ZHC by sending 2 (Part 3). Innr's report was for a single bulb; the group case was reported failing in 2020 and the fix later changed (`allEqual`); current group behavior unverified.
- Tuya-inside (Lidl 14156506L, 14158804L, HG08010, HG07834A/B/C, Nous P3Z): levels 1 and 2 leave the bulb physically on while Z2M shows off; an off with a transition or a turn-on above 2 flickers or turns it off again ([Z2M #21286](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/21286), closed 2025-09-30, the thread shows no fix; [Z2M #27498](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/27498); [Z2M #33465](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/33465), 2026: `currentLevel` drops to 1 on OFF and bulbs come on at about 1%). Koenkk on a similar eWeLink-branded Tuya bulb: "a known issue with some Tuya devices, they don't deal well with the `moveToLevelWithOnOff` command" ([Z2M #29996](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/29996)). ZHC's fix is `moveToLevelWithOnOffDisable` on 5 Tuya definitions ([ZHC #12783](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/pull/12783), named in [Z2M #33465](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/33465)), which still leaves #33465's bulbs dim.
- eWeLink YSR-MINI / ZB-DL01: firmware bugs with `moveToLevelWithOnOff`, "incomplete OFF + ignored transition for ON" ([ZHC #12248](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/issues/12248)); ZHC `ysrsai.ts` is `tuyaLight`, so placement follows Part 6 (strings unchecked: unverified).

### 4c. Soft off (lower to 0)

- ZHC's "off with a transition" is a `moveToLevelWithOnOff` to 0, not an Off ([L1305-L1311](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/03b37655f540668d94975c47550f249fc0d16e26/src/converters/toZigbee.ts#L1305)). Z2M's own maintainers: on/off have no transition parameter, "we replace them with: dim (to minimum, off) and brighten (to previous brightness). This behavior is very problematic and it should be removed" and the stored level can be left at 1 ([Z2M #33477](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/33477), open, Hue and IKEA examples); "it's not guaranteed to work" ([Z2M #31956](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/31956)). The known failures are AwoX and the Tuya bulbs above, and IKEA's family (already outside `other`; [Hue internals](../../hue/internals.md)).
- Consequence for a user of `other`: lowering to 0 can leave a light on at the lowest level, and the next plain `turn_on` can come up dim ("Kept levels" in [docs/internals.md](../../../internals.md#shared) covers what DeviceSide does about the kept level). Visible, one tap.

### 4d. Stop

- No report found of a non-Hue light ignoring Stop or running on after it (searches: "brightness_move stop", per vendor). The old reports are of the converter, not a light: `state` sent alongside `brightness_move` restores the old level ([Z2M #3456](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/3456), 2020, Hue and IKEA; DeviceSide sends the key alone); a stop to a group publishes the first member's state for all ([ZHC #2997](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/issues/2997), handled by DeviceSide's per-member reads).
- [Z2M #33321](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/issues/33321): after `brightness_move: 0` Z2M's `brightness` "stays at the same value" on a LEDVANCE Panel TW Z3 and a Hue (Z2M 2.14.2, open). DeviceSide's stop sends `"stop"`, the same branch of the converter, and takes Z2M's read-back as right where no move of ours ran (`stop.py`). So HA can show a stale brightness after a stop on such a light: unverified for `"stop"`.
- Stop's read-back failure turns a delivered stop into `rejected`/`send_failed` through `set_check` (Part 1b): code reading, no report.

## Part 5: Can a plug, switch or relay in `other` reach these actions?

- **Domain.** `Z2MRamp.scope` and `Z2MStop.scope` use `domains=verified(Platform.LIGHT)` ([fade.py `ramp_scope`](../../../../custom_components/deviceside/integrations/zigbee2mqtt/fade.py), [stop.py](../../../../custom_components/deviceside/integrations/zigbee2mqtt/stop.py)). `other` is declared for light and switch ([classes.py `CLASSES`](../../../../custom_components/deviceside/integrations/zigbee2mqtt/classes.py)), but the adapters' domains win: a `switch` entity is `domain_not_supported` (named raises, indirect skips; [core/dispatcher.py](../../../../custom_components/deviceside/core/dispatcher.py) L308-L326). Z2M shows plugs as switches. `classes.py`'s `_CERTIFIED = labs(OTHER)` carries no domains because it doesn't need them; `get_attributes`/`set_attributes` narrow to lights with `only=True`.
- **A `switch_as_x` light** is "served as the switch it wraps" ([docs/internals.md](../../../internals.md#shared)), so it never reaches the dimming adapters, and `fields.lacking` says a switch, or a switch a `switch_as_x` light drives, lacks every field ([core/fields.py L42-L60](../../../../custom_components/deviceside/core/fields.py)).
- **An on/off-only `light`** (a relay or LED switch Z2M exposes as a light): the state lists `onoff` alone, `lacking` drops `brightness`, and the raise or lower is `unchanged` with `fields_dropped` (dispatcher docstring). A `stop` has no fields, so `{"brightness_move": "stop"}` goes out; Z2M logs `No converter available for 'brightness_move'` and carries on ([publish.ts L207-L210](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/blob/3c841fdfd66c92239921ef84fde8f039c64e1662/lib/extension/publish.ts#L207)); DeviceSide's `set_check` waits for a "Publish ... failed" line that this isn't, so it answers `sent`. Harmless. Whether a state that omits `supported_color_modes` (`lacking` then drops nothing) occurs on Z2M lights: unverified; HA's MQTT light always lists modes.
- **A dimmer wired to a load** (EcoDim, Aqara H2, Namron) is a light and is served. A fade on a mains dimmer failing shows as the lamp not following. Harm is the user's mains load being at a level they didn't want, visible at once. Low-level flicker on LED loads: unverified.

## Part 6: Tuya-inside lights in `other` before bridge/devices arrives

- **The window.** Until Z2M's `bridge/devices` lists a device with `interview_completed` and both Zigbee strings (or, for a device whose interview never completes, for good), a Tuya device under another vendor's definition lands in `other`: [classes.py](../../../../custom_components/deviceside/integrations/zigbee2mqtt/classes.py) names Lidl's `HG06337` as the example, because the registry rules see only vendor `Tuya` or a model starting `TS`. Core treats it as that class for every action but one whose call harms ([Unplaced](../../../../custom_components/deviceside/integrations/zigbee2mqtt/classes.py)); dimming doesn't harm, so a fade is served in Labs.
- **After placing**: `_TZ\d{4}_` strings or `TS\d{4}` models match `tuya_zcl` ([zigbee/tuya.py](../../../../custom_components/deviceside/zigbee/tuya.py), `ZCL_MANUFACTURER_PATTERN`, `ZCL_MODEL_PATTERN`); the Tuya family's capabilities hold no entry for `raise_brightness`, `lower_brightness` or `stop` (rg over `families/`: only Hue, IKEA and Sonoff name them), so they are refused as not built. Tuya-inside lights under another vendor (Lidl, EcoDim ED-10041/10042, eWeLink/YSR, Immax, Woox, Skydance, Mercator, Müller; `rg -c "tuyaLight("` over the pinned ZHC finds 25 in `tuya.ts` and 13 other files) are expected to carry a Tuya `_TZ...` string, so they leave `other` once strings arrive (not checked model by model: unverified).
- **What a fade does in the window.** The first step is level 1 at transition 0, then the fade. The evidence above (4b) says a TS0505B holds level 1 or 2 as off-but-on, and `moveToLevelWithOnOff` is the command Tuya firmware handles worst. So a Tuya-inside bulb in the window can end a raise on at the bottom, or flash, or fail to turn off after a lower to 0. Visible, one tap. Duration of the window: on the order of the bridge's connect (the retained `bridge/devices`), plus any device whose interview doesn't finish; not measured here.
- **Options if the window should be closed:** make the three adapters refuse an Unplaced `other` (as `turn_on_for` does), or accept it as is. This note doesn't recommend closing it: the failure shows at once and the window is short, and tenet 6 asks for evidence that a guard's failure happens; the only evidence is for TS0505B bulbs, whose own placement refuses them.
