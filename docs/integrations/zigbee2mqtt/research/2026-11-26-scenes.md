# Zigbee2MQTT scenes: what Home Assistant and MQTT expose (2026-11-26)

Research date: 2026-11-26. Scope: whether a Zigbee2MQTT scene is a Home Assistant scene entity, and whether its
contents (each light's stored settings) can be read without recalling it, so `set_scene` could write them with
`set_attributes` as it does an HA scene. Spec: `docs/plans/2026-11-26-set-scene-ha-scenes.md`, step 1. Sources
pinned to Zigbee2MQTT [`2.14.3`](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/tree/2.14.3) (latest release), the
zigbee-herdsman-converters [`v26.125.0`](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/tree/v26.125.0) and
zigbee-herdsman [`v11.2.0`](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman/tree/v11.2.0) its `package.json` pins, and HA
core [`2026.11.3`](https://github.com/home-assistant/core/tree/2026.11.3). Source reading only; no probe.

## 1. Zigbee2MQTT scenes are HA scene entities, command-only

- Zigbee2MQTT's HA extension discovers one `scene` entity per stored scene, on each device endpoint and each group:
  object_id `scene_<id>`, `state_topic: false`, `command_topic` the entity's `/set` topic, and
  `payload_on: '{ "scene_recall": <id> }'`
  ([homeassistant.ts#L1740-L1757](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/blob/2.14.3/lib/extension/homeassistant.ts#L1740-L1757);
  the `state_topic: false` branch, [#L1815-L1823](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/blob/2.14.3/lib/extension/homeassistant.ts#L1815-L1823)).
  Its unique_id is `<ieee>_scene_<id>_<base topic>` on the pattern of every Z2M entity
  ([#L1853-L1855](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/blob/2.14.3/lib/extension/homeassistant.ts#L1853-L1855)).
- Discovery is redone whenever a scene converter runs (`scene_store`, `scene_add`, `scene_remove`,
  `scene_remove_all`, `scene_rename`)
  ([publish.ts#L291-L304](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/blob/2.14.3/lib/extension/publish.ts#L291-L304)).
- Only scenes in Zigbee2MQTT's own endpoint meta are listed (`getScenes`,
  [utils.ts#L362-L378](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/blob/2.14.3/lib/util/utils.ts#L362-L378)): a scene
  stored on the device by a remote, another controller or a vendor app has no entity.
- HA's MQTT scene platform takes `command_topic`, `payload_on` and `retain`, subscribes to nothing, and its
  `async_activate` only publishes `payload_on`
  ([mqtt/scene.py#L29-L36, #L77-L94](https://github.com/home-assistant/core/blob/2026.11.3/homeassistant/components/mqtt/scene.py#L29-L94)).
  The entity's platform is `mqtt`, not `homeassistant`, and it holds no contents.

## 2. A scene's contents can't be read without recalling it

- **Bridge topics:** `bridge/devices` (per endpoint) and `bridge/groups` list scenes as `{id, name}` only;
  `getScenes` drops the stored state
  ([utils.ts#L362-L378](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/blob/2.14.3/lib/util/utils.ts#L362-L378),
  [api.ts#L229-L232](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/blob/2.14.3/lib/types/api.ts#L229-L232),
  [bridge.ts#L829-L840](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/blob/2.14.3/lib/extension/bridge.ts#L829-L840),
  [#L893-L911](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/blob/2.14.3/lib/extension/bridge.ts#L893-L911)).
- **Bridge requests:** none handles scenes
  ([bridge.ts#L33-L60](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee2mqtt/blob/2.14.3/lib/extension/bridge.ts#L33-L60)).
- **`database.db`:** the contents live here only. `saveSceneState` writes
  `endpoint.meta.scenes["<sceneID>_<groupID>"] = {name, state}`, `state` filtered to `state`, `brightness`,
  `color`, `color_temp` and `color_mode`
  ([zhc utils.ts#L459-L465](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/v26.125.0/src/lib/utils.ts#L459-L465)),
  and herdsman persists `meta` with the endpoint
  ([endpoint.ts#L280-L297](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman/blob/v11.2.0/src/controller/model/endpoint.ts#L280-L297)).
  It is Zigbee2MQTT's copy, not the device's: `scene_store` saves Zigbee2MQTT's cached last-known state (a group's,
  its cached member states), and `scene_add` saves the requested values to every group member, whether or not the
  member has each attribute
  ([toZigbee.ts#L2293-L2328, #L2387-L2536](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/v26.125.0/src/converters/toZigbee.ts#L2293-L2536)).
  Nothing re-syncs it with the device's scene table. The file is on Zigbee2MQTT's host, which HA often can't reach.
- **`scene_recall`** sends `genScenes.recall` and then publishes the stored copy as the entity's new state
  ([toZigbee.ts#L2330-L2385](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/v26.125.0/src/converters/toZigbee.ts#L2330-L2385)):
  the contents reach MQTT only by recalling the scene, which turns the lights on, the opposite of `set_scene`.
- **View Scene:** zigbee-herdsman defines `view` and `enhancedView` and their responses
  ([cluster.ts#L309](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman/blob/v11.2.0/src/zspec/zcl/definition/cluster.ts#L309)),
  but no generic converter sends them: the genScenes converters are store, recall, add, remove, remove_all and rename
  ([index.ts#L424-L429](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/v26.125.0/src/index.ts#L424-L429)).
  Unverified: per-device definitions under `src/devices` weren't searched for a custom View Scene.

## 3. Conclusion

Not buildable today. A Zigbee2MQTT scene is an HA `scene` entity on the `mqtt` platform, so it can be targeted, but
nothing HA or MQTT exposes holds its contents: only `database.db` does, as Zigbee2MQTT's own copy, and the one path
that publishes them is a recall. It would open with an upstream change that publishes `meta.scenes` (an enriched
`getScenes`, or a bridge request that returns a scene's state), or a converter that sends View Scene and reports the
reply. No issue filed.
