## Setup

- Device: Z2M definition `T2_E27` ("E27 RGB led bulb"), vendor `Aqara`, Zigbee model `lumi.light.agl003`,
  manufacturer `Aqara`, `date_code` `20260605` (Z2M lists no `software_build_id`), router, mains. Endpoint 1 input
  clusters: genScenes, genGroups, genIdentify, genBasic, lightingColorCtrl, genLevelCtrl, genOnOff,
  haElectricalMeasurement, seMetering, manuSpecificLumi; output genOta, genTime. Endpoint 21: genAnalogInput. No
  bindings and no configured reportings in `bridge/devices`.
- Its Z2M state carries Aqara's own fades: `on_off_duration` and `off_on_duration` 1.5 s, and genLevelCtrl
  `onOffTransitionTime` reads 15 (tenths). So a plain on or off fades over 1.5 s on the bulb.
- Home Assistant 2026.10.4, Zigbee2MQTT 2.14.3, zigbee-herdsman-converters 26.125.0, zigbee-herdsman 11.2.0,
  EmberZNet coordinator. DeviceSide 0.2.0 as deployed on the rig, which places the bulb in class `other` (the
  deployed build predates the `aqara` family).
- A Python script on ha-rig's `HAClient` (admin token) published to the bulb's `set` and `get` topics through HA's
  `mqtt.publish`, and logged with a local timestamp each payload on the bulb's state topic and each line on
  `zigbee2mqtt/bridge/logging`. Reads used Z2M's generic `read` payload with `state_property: deviceside_read` (in
  the bulb's `filtered_cache` already), one attribute per read, since one unsupported attribute fails a whole read.
  Raw ZCL went as Z2M's generic `command` payload (`{"command": {"cluster", "command", "payload"}}`). Not run under
  the rig's lock; no rig run was going (every rig lock free at the start).
- Z2M's log level was `warning` (probes 1 to 3) and `debug` for probe 4, put back to `warning` after.
  `advanced.log_debug_to_mqtt_frontend` is `false` and was not changed, so no `zh:` debug line reached the MQTT log
  feed: probe 4 got Z2M's `info` lines (its MQTT publishes) and errors only. HA's `deviceside` logger ran at DEBUG for
  probe 4 (back to WARNING after), for DeviceSide's per-call lines.
- Start state: off; Z2M's cache level 102, color mode color temperature, 240 mireds. Put back at the end (off, level
  102, colorMode 2, 239 mireds: a 1-mired rounding through HA's kelvin).

## 3. genOnOff onWithTimedOff (`on_time`, writes)

DeviceSide's own call (`turn_on_for`): `{"state": "ON", "on_time": N}` to the `set` topic, which ZHC's generic
`tz.on_off` sends as `genOnOff.onWithTimedOff {ctrlbits: 0, ontime: round(N × 10), offwaittime: 0}`, `on_time: 0`
included (the branch tests `on_time != null`;
[toZigbee.ts L16-L54](https://github.com/Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters/blob/afdaa028a0972d801da762196ddfb2df631ea0cc/src/converters/toZigbee.ts#L16-L54);
no override for Lumi lights, [timed-on research](../2026-11-14-timed-on-call-patterns.md)). Each case started from
off (`light.turn_off`, then 4 s). **Measured** by a `{"state": ""}` publish to the `get` topic every 1.0 s (a
genOnOff OnOff read) from the timer's publish to the case's end, each reply's state payload timestamped on arrival;
the bulb sends no report of its own (no reporting configured). Every read was answered (25, 43 and 29 replies), so the
state is known to within 1 s throughout. Times are seconds from the first publish.

## 4. The `no genLevelCtrl reply` timeout (writes)

**What hit it on 2026-11-24.** Not a case's setup step: the rig's put-back after `raise_saturation`'s
`saturation_stop_raise[light_color]` (ha-rig `Snapshot.put_back`). That case's setup turned the bulb on
(`brightness_pct: 70, hs_color: [200, 20]`), waited 1 s, and failed at once at its first call (`not_built`). The
put-back then calls `light.turn_off` and, with no wait, `deviceside.set_attributes` with the attributes the rig had
read from the off bulb at the case's start (`brightness: 40.2, color_temp_kelvin: 2702`). DeviceSide sent
`moveToLevel {level: 102, transtime: 4, optionsMask: 1, optionsOverride: 1}`, `moveToColorTemp {colortemp: 370, ...}`
and Z2M's own kept-level `{"state": null, "brightness": 102, "transition": 0.4}`; its read-back of CurrentLevel got
no reply in 5 s (HA 23:31:40.04, `TimeoutError('no genLevelCtrl reply from Zigbee2MQTT')`), and Z2M logged the
moveToLevel timed out after 10 s. The run's journal kept the bulb as `unrestored`. Report:
`rig-run-2026-11-24-232946.md`, its `.ha.log` and `.z2m.log` (rig temp folder).

**Repro.** Each run: `light.turn_on {brightness_pct: 70, hs_color: [200, 20]}`, wait for HA's brightness 176 to
180, wait 1 s, then the step, then 1 s, check `off`, `light.turn_on {}`, 3 s, read HA's state; about 8 s a run.

What it shows:

- The bulb sent no default response to the override moveToLevel within herdsman's 10 s (`disableDefaultResponse:

- The CurrentLevel read DeviceSide sent after its fade wait was answered only at 09:07:03.465, 0.27 s after the

- A light whose hung moveToLevel holds its later requests answers DeviceSide's read late, so `no genLevelCtrl reply`

Counts: 1 timeout in 20 runs; 0 in 10 with HA's `off` awaited, 1 in 10 with `set_attributes` sent at once after
`light.turn_off`, as the rig's put-back does. The 2026-11-24 one was the put-back too. The bulb was on (level 177)
and starting its 1.5 s off fade in each.
